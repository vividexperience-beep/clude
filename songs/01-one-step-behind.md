# One Step Behind

テーマ: すれ違う関係の温度差
ジャンル系統: シグネチャー系(Future Garage + Japanese Pop + Uk Garage Groove)
特殊構成: エコー・ズレ二重唱(リードボーカルに対し、半歩遅れて自分の言葉で応じるエコーボーカルが最後まで完全には重ならない)

## スタイルプロンプト

```
Powerful Female Vocal,
Front And Center Vocal,
Strong Vocal Presence,
Emotionally Restrained Delivery,

Future Garage,
Japanese Pop,
Uk Garage Groove,
Syncopated Garage Rhythm,

128 Bpm,

Deep Sub Kick,
Skippy Hi-Hat Pattern,
Flowing Sub Bass,

Cold Synth Atmosphere,
Shimmering Synth Textures,
Sparse Arrangement In Verses,

Two Vocal Characters, Lead And Echo,
Echo Vocal Answers With Its Own Short Reactive Lines,
Echo Vocal Enters Slightly Late, Never Fully Synced With Lead,
Echo Vocal Absent From Final Resolution,

Wide Stereo Mix,
Dynamic Contrast,
Tension And Release,
Bittersweet Energy,

Instrumental Garage Drop,

Clean Modern Production,
No Fade Out,
Not Ballad,
Not Ambient,
No Crowd Vocals,
Avoid Resolved Happy Ending
```
